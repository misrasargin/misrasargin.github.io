How I work

Start from a question

A project begins with something a person would actually ask, not with a dataset.

Show the uncertainty

Every result comes with an interval or a test, and with a check on data the model has not seen.

Say where it breaks

Each report ends with what the analysis cannot tell you.

Make it reproducible

Data is downloaded by script, seeds are fixed, and the numbers in the text are read from the output.
